Features

Everything you reach for, every day.

The workflow you know from GitKraken, rebuilt from scratch as a native app with nothing in between you and your repository.

A graph you can read

Every branch, remote, tag and stash in one colorful, topologically ordered graph — with HEAD and your uncommitted work right on top.

Stage lines, not just files

Click the exact lines you want, or stage, unstage and discard whole hunks. Perfect commits without touching the terminal.

Native speed

Rust from top to bottom, GPU-rendered with gpui. History comes straight from gitoxide, so even giant repos open in seconds.

Right-click anything

Checkout, branch, tag, merge, rebase, cherry-pick, revert and reset straight from the graph, the sidebar or the file list.

Always up to date

Edit in your editor, commit in the terminal — GitChrono notices instantly, and stays quiet while it's in the background.

Behaves exactly like git

Writes go through your own git, so hooks, SSH keys, credential helpers and commit signing all just work.

How it works

Fast where it matters, faithful everywhere else.

  • 1
    Reads in-process with gitoxideHistory walks, refs and commit trees are read directly from the object database — no subprocess per commit.
  • 2
    Writes through your gitCommit, push, rebase and stash run your installed git, so every repository behaves exactly like it does in the terminal.
  • 3
    Painted on the GPUThe graph and lists are virtualized and drawn with gpui, the framework behind the Zed editor.
// gitchrono-core: the history walk
let walk = topo::Builder::new(&repo.objects)
    .with_tips(tips)
    .sorting(topo::Sorting::TopoOrder)
    .with_commit_graph(repo.commit_graph_if_enabled()?)
    .build()?;
