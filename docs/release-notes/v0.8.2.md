> [!WARNING]
> **GitChrono is in alpha. Use at your own risk.** It runs real git commands on your repositories, so keep a pushed remote or a backup of anything you can't afford to lose.

## What's new in 0.8.2

**Clearer while staging**
- While files are being staged, a small spinner turns next to **Stage all changes** (or **Unstage all changes** when unstaging), instead of "Staging…" at the top of the window
- The stage and unstage buttons are greyed out until it's done, so it's clear another click won't do anything yet

**Colors**
- Modified files show a yellow **M**, as in GitKraken and like the ✎ counts on the WIP row and the Home tab
- Conflicted files are now red, so they stand out from modified ones

## Install

| Platform | File |
|---|---|
| macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel) | `GitChrono-0.8.2-macos-universal.dmg` |
| Windows 64-bit | `GitChrono-0.8.2-windows-x86_64.zip` (Git included) |
| Linux x86_64 / ARM64 | `GitChrono-0.8.2-linux-*.tar.gz` |

Already on 0.3.0 or newer? GitChrono offers this update itself. Step-by-step instructions are on [gitchrono.com/download](https://gitchrono.com/download/).

Requires `git` 2.40 or newer (included on Windows).
